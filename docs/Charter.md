## 2. SCOPE

AVCS applies wherever decisions carry operational risk and where delayed action creates irreversible or accumulating consequences.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- maritime and offshore operations,
- energy and oil & gas,
- aviation,
- high-risk industrial systems,
- infrastructure projects,
- any environment where failure has material consequences.

AVCS operates at the structural layer of authority, escalation, and accountability.

It does not replace technical standards, HSE systems, or operational procedures.

It evaluates the decision integrity of the system that operates those procedures.

## 3. CORE PRINCIPLES

The following six principles are foundational. They are operationally anchored. They are not subject to silent reinterpretation.

### 3.1 Structural Clarity Over Reactive Correction

We prioritize architectural integrity over post-incident explanation.

Root Cause Analysis explains failure. Structural design prevents silent drift.

### 3.2 Mandatory Triggers

Critical thresholds must be defined as mandatory, not interpretive.

If a condition requires action, it must structurally require action.

### 3.3 Singular Decision Ownership

Responsibility for critical decisions must be explicit and non-diffused.

Shared awareness is valuable. Diffused ownership is structural weakness.

### 3.4 Protected Intervention

The system must protect those who intervene early.

If intervention carries personal cost, drift will accumulate.

### 3.5 Override Transparency

All overrides must be visible, logged, and reviewable.

Invisible overrides erode structural integrity.

### 3.6 Drift Detection

Deviation from baseline must be structurally detectable.

Normalization without detection is architectural decay.

## 4. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

Beyond the six core principles, the following principles are foundational to AVCS. They emerged from structural testing of AVCS on itself, and from independent convergence with other professional architectures.

### 4.1 Code Implements the Constitution — Not Redefines It

Code must implement the Constitution — not redefine it.

No implementation — whether software, procedure, or organizational practice — may silently redefine the meaning of a foundational role, principle, or boundary.

### 4.2 Divergence Becomes a Test Result — Not an Automatic Constitutional Change

If implementation and Constitution diverge, the divergence becomes a **test result** — not an automatic Constitutional change.

The Constitution defines what each role is. The implementation must conform to that definition — not replace it.

### 4.3 A Status Is Not a Structural Condition

**A status is not a structural condition.**

A structural condition must be **independently verifiable from evidence**.

This principle applies across all operational states. For each critical state, three things must be independently establishable:

1. The **claimed status** — what the record says.
2. The **required structural condition** — what must actually be true.
3. The **evidence** — what can be independently verified.

If the condition behind the status cannot be shown, the status is not structural evidence.

| Claimed Status | Required Structural Condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Protected** | Intervention can actually be exercised without structural penalty | Pre-existing protection + authority + trace |
| **Authorized** | Explicit authority exists at the decision point | Actor + authority basis + timestamp |
| **Controlled** | System can stop when required | Available intervention path + boundary |
| **Within North** | Governing boundary is determinate and applicable | Authoritative boundary + evidence |
| **Closed** | Required corrective condition has been achieved | Retrievable artifact + independent verification |

The diagnostic question is:

> **Show me the condition behind the status.**

If the condition cannot be shown, the status is not structural.

### 4.4 Evidence Ex Ante

Evidence establishing a decision condition, authority, boundary, or protected intervention must exist **before or at the moment** of the act it governs.

Evidence of execution or outcome may necessarily arise afterward, but it must not be used to **retroactively establish** the legitimacy of the decision, authority, boundary, or protection that existed at the time.

A structural condition must not be established retrospectively from evidence that did not exist or was not traceable at the decision point.

This principle applies to:

- **Intervention** — the protection of intervention must be verifiable at the moment of the decision, not reconstructed after the consequence.
- **Close-out** — closure criteria must be written when the action is raised, not when it is closed.
- **Authority** — authorization must be recorded at the moment it is given, not reconstructed afterward.
- **Boundary** — the state of North must be determinate at the moment continuation is authorized, not inferred later.

### 4.5 North Is Applied, Not Interpreted

**North is not interpreted. North is applied.**

North is the non-negotiable boundary. It cannot be silently reinterpreted, optimized away, or overridden without becoming visible.

When the boundary itself becomes contested — for example, when two functions read the same limit differently — the divergence is treated as a **structural signal**, not as an interpretive dispute.

The affected continuation path is **frozen** until the governing boundary is re-established as sufficiently **determinate, authoritative, and applicable to the affected operation**.

The system does not decide which professional interpretation is "right." It makes the disagreement visible, identifies the governing source of authority, and determines whether continuation is still authorized.

### 4.6 Human Sovereignty

No system has authority over the human who carries the consequence.

AI may analyze. AI may recommend. Authority must remain explicit. Execution must be controlled. The decision pathway must remain visible.

The purpose of this boundary is to prevent analytical capability from being confused with operational authority.

### 4.7 Structural Condition Must Be Independently Verifiable

A structural condition exists only if it can be **independently verified** from evidence.

Verification must not depend on the later cooperation or honesty of the party whose conduct is in question.

Verification must survive:

- staff rotation,
- shift changes,
- organizational restructuring,
- and the passage of time.

What survives a year of rotation is evidence. What does not was status.

## 5. NON-NEGOTIABLES

AVCS rejects, as structurally incompatible with its principles:

- Blame-based simplification.
- KPI substitution for structural health.
- Interpretive safety thresholds where mandatory thresholds are required.
- Hidden authority structures.
- Cultural normalization of silent risk.
- **Status substituted for structural condition.**
- **Evidence reconstructed after the consequence.**
- **Boundary treated as negotiable when contested.**
- **Recommendation treated as authorization.**
- **Continuation authorized without the structural ability to stop.**

Structural weakness cannot be compensated indefinitely by human effort.

## 9. EVOLUTION CLAUSE

AVCS is a living framework. Versioning reflects learning, not ideological drift.

Structural principles may be refined. The following remain foundational and are not subject to amendment without a formal Constitutional process:

- Human Sovereignty.
- North is applied, not interpreted.
- A status is not a structural condition.
- Evidence ex ante.
- Code implements the Constitution — not redefines it.

### 9.1 Amendment Process

Substantive changes require:

1. Documentation of the reason for change.
2. Identification of the source architecture (if the change originated from joint work or external convergence).
3. Preservation of provenance.
4. Review by the AVCS Practitioner Council (once established).
5. Two-thirds majority approval.

### 9.2 Provenance

Where AVCS adopts a principle, formulation, or structural distinction that originated outside AVCS, the origin is preserved.

**Convergence is recorded, not absorbed.**

### 9.3 Versioning

- **Minor version** (v1.x) — clarification, expansion, addition of non-foundational principles.
- **Major version** (v2.x) — change to foundational principles. Requires full Constitutional process.
