# AVCS CODE OF ETHICS

## Principles for Professional Conduct

**Version:** 1.1
**Status:** Binding Professional Standard — Frozen
**Effective Date:** September 2026
**Supersedes:** Code of Ethics v1.0 (February 2026)
**Companion Documents:** AVCS Charter v1.1, AVCS CORE v2.1

## PREAMBLE

AVCS was built for one purpose: to protect human judgment in environments where the cost of failure is measured in lives, environment, and trust.

Our strength is not in tools or methodology alone. It is in the trust that our work will be used to strengthen systems — not to harm people.

This Code of Ethics defines the line we do not cross.

It is subordinate to the **AVCS Charter v1.1**. Within the ethical domain, where the Charter and this Code overlap, the more restrictive ethical obligation applies.

It applies to every individual who holds AVCS certification.

**Violation of this Code may result in sanctions up to and including revocation of certification and exclusion from the community.**

It is not optional. It is the conscience of the community.

---

## 1. RESPECT FOR HUMAN DIGNITY

**1.1** We recognize that every operational system is composed of humans doing their best within imperfect structures. Our work honors that reality.

**1.2** We never use AVCS data, findings, or reports to evaluate, punish, or justify action against specific individuals.

**1.3** We protect the anonymity of all personnel who share their experiences during audits. No identifiable information enters reports without explicit, written consent.

**1.4** We acknowledge that behind every structural weakness are decisions made by people operating under constraints they did not design.

**1.5** We treat every person we encounter — operator, supervisor, manager, executive — with the same respect, regardless of role or seniority.

---

## 2. TRUTH BEFORE CONVENIENCE

**2.1** We do not suppress or soften findings to please clients, protect relationships, or secure future work.

**2.2** We clearly distinguish between:

- **Facts** — observable, documented, verifiable.
- **Interpretations** — our professional judgment based on facts.
- **Recommendations** — suggested actions based on both.

**2.3** We do not overstate precision. SIM scores are indicators, not absolute measurements. This limitation is stated explicitly in every report.

**2.4** When evidence is ambiguous or contradictory, we say so. We do not pretend certainty.

**2.5** We distinguish between what we know, what we infer, and what we assume. We do not present assumption as knowledge.

---

## 3. INDEPENDENCE AND IMPARTIALITY

**3.1** We do not accept engagements where our objectivity could reasonably be questioned.

**3.2** We do not conduct audits in organizations where we have been employed within the preceding two years, without review and waiver by the Practitioner Council.

**3.3** We do not accept compensation tied to audit outcomes, scores, or client satisfaction metrics.

**3.4** If asked to alter findings, omit vulnerabilities, or reframe conclusions to suit commercial or political interests, we refuse. If pressure persists, we withdraw from the engagement.

**3.5** We disclose any relationship — financial, personal, or professional — that could be perceived as a conflict of interest.

---

## 4. ACCOUNTABILITY FOR CONSEQUENCES

**4.1** We recognize that our reports may influence investment decisions, operational changes, and professional careers. We write with that weight in mind.

**4.2** We formulate findings and recommendations in ways that cannot reasonably be misused to target individuals.

**4.3** If we become aware that our work is being used in ways inconsistent with this Code — including for retaliation, blame, or unfair dismissal — we:

- Raise the concern with the client.
- If unresolved, notify the Practitioner Council.
- In extreme cases, publicly disassociate from the misuse.

**4.4** We accept responsibility for our own errors. When we make mistakes, we acknowledge them and correct them.

**4.5** We do not use our position to advance personal interests at the expense of the profession.

---

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA INTEGRITY

**5.1** We treat all client data as confidential unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing.

**5.2** We do not share raw interview notes, internal documents, or draft findings with third parties.

**5.3** Anonymized case studies may be developed only with client permission and must be reviewed to ensure no individual or operation can be identified.

**5.4** We store audit records securely and retain them only as long as necessary for professional or legal purposes.

**5.5** Where data is used for research, it is anonymized and aggregated before analysis.

---

## 6. PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

**6.1** We do not represent ourselves as AVCS Practitioners if our certification has lapsed or been revoked.

**6.2** We do not claim AVCS affiliation for work that has not followed the Code of Practice or that violates this Code.

**6.3** We do not use the AVCS name or brand to imply endorsement of products, services, or organizations without explicit authorization.

**6.4** We avoid conflicts of interest. If a conflict arises, we disclose it immediately and, if necessary, withdraw.

**6.5** We do not offer AVCS services outside our competence. Where specialist knowledge is required, we refer to qualified professionals.

---

## 7. COMMITMENT TO GROWTH AND COMMUNITY

**7.1** We share knowledge with the community. We contribute to the evolution of the methodology.

**7.2** We treat fellow Practitioners with respect, even in disagreement.

**7.3** We mentor new Practitioners when asked and when capable.

**7.4** We welcome critique of our own work. We believe architecture strengthens under scrutiny.

**7.5** We acknowledge the contributions of others. We do not absorb external ideas without attribution.

---

## 8. VIOLATIONS AND SANCTIONS

**8.1** Alleged violations of this Code are reviewed by the AVCS Practitioner Council.

**8.2** The accused Practitioner has the right to present their perspective before any decision.

**8.3** Sanctions may include:

| Sanction | Description |
|---|---|
| **Warning** | Written notice of concern. |
| **Probation** | Conditional certification with specified corrective actions. |
| **Suspension** | Temporary loss of certification (up to 12 months). |
| **Revocation** | Permanent loss of certification and exclusion from the community. |

**8.4** Revocation requires:

- A finding of a serious or repeated violation.
- A two-thirds majority of the Council.

**8.5** Revocation is made public (removal from the directory). The reasons remain confidential unless the Practitioner chooses to disclose them.

---

## 9. ETHICAL OBLIGATION OF THE FOUNDER

**9.1** The Founder of AVCS is bound by this Code in the same manner as every certified Practitioner.

**9.2** Allegations against the Founder are reviewed by a panel of three senior Practitioners selected by the Council.

**9.3** If the Founder is found to have violated the Code, the same sanctions apply — including potential revocation of Founder privileges in community governance.

**9.4** The Founder is not the final authority in matters of ethics. The Practitioner Council is.

---

## 10. ADOPTION AND EVOLUTION

**10.1** Every certified AVCS Practitioner must formally acknowledge and commit to this Code upon certification and at each recertification.

**10.2** This Code may be amended by:

- The Founder, during the initial phase (first 10 Practitioners).
- The Practitioner Council, by two-thirds majority, thereafter.

**10.3** Substantive changes require:

- Documentation of the reason for change.
- Identification of the source architecture (if the change originated from joint work or external convergence).
- Preservation of provenance.
- Communication to all active Practitioners.
- Reaffirmation by all active Practitioners.

**10.4** This Code is a living document. It will evolve as the community of Practitioners grows. Its core principles — respect, truth, independence, and accountability — remain permanent.

---

## 11. EVIDENCE BEFORE STATUS

**11.1** Practitioners must not accept a claimed status — **Protected, Authorized, Controlled, Within North, Closed** — as evidence of a structural condition.

**11.2** A structural condition must be independently verifiable from evidence that existed at or before the moment of the act it governs.

**11.3** For every critical state, Practitioners must establish:

- The **claimed status** — what the record says.
- The **required structural condition** — what must actually be true.
- The **evidence** — what can be independently verified.

**11.4** If the condition behind the status cannot be shown, the Practitioner must report the status as **unsubstantiated**.

**11.5** The diagnostic question is:

> **Show me the condition behind the status.**

---

## 12. AI AND HUMAN AUTHORITY

**12.1** Practitioners may use AI as an analytical instrument. AI may assist with data analysis, pattern detection, and structural assessment.

**12.2** AI must not authorize. AI must not execute. AI must not replace human judgment.

**12.3** Where AI is used, the Practitioner remains fully responsible for the findings, conclusions, and recommendations.

**12.4** Practitioners must not present AI-generated analysis as human professional judgment.

**12.5** Recommendation is not authority. Analysis is not authorization. Capability is not permission.

---

## 13. OPERATOR FITNESS PROTECTION

**13.1** Practitioners must recognize that human performance depends on conditions that may be structurally compromised — fatigue, illness, intoxication, mental distress.

**13.2** Practitioners must not use AVCS findings to evaluate or penalize individual operators for conditions arising from structural inadequacy.

**13.3** Practitioners must ensure that the architecture they assess includes protected mechanisms for an operator to refuse duty when unfit.

**13.4** Where such mechanisms are absent, the absence is reported as a structural gap — not as an individual performance issue.

---

## 14. PRACTITIONER COUNCIL

**14.1** AVCS establishes a **Practitioner Council** responsible for:

- Reviewing alleged violations of this Code.
- Establishing procedures for certification and recertification.
- Adjudicating appeals.
- Maintaining the public directory of certified Practitioners.
- Amending the Code of Ethics when required.

**14.2** Until the Council is formally established, its functions are carried out by the Founder — except in matters concerning the Founder, which are adjudicated by a panel of three senior Practitioners.

**14.3** The composition, procedures, and mandate of the Council are defined in a separate document: **AVCS Practitioner Council Charter**.

---

## 15. RELATIONSHIP TO THE CODE OF PRACTICE

**15.1** This Code of Ethics defines professional conduct. The **AVCS Code of Practice** defines the technical standard for SIM audits.

**15.2** Both are binding on certified Practitioners.

**15.3** Where a matter concerns both ethics and technical procedure, both documents apply.

**15.4** Where this Code and the Code of Practice appear to conflict, this Code prevails in ethical matters. The Code of Practice prevails in technical matters.

---

## 16. RESEARCH PARTICIPATION

**16.1** Where Practitioners participate in AVCS research — including joint boundary tests, drift detection, and convergence studies — the following principles apply:

- Findings must be provisional until tested.
- Convergence must be recorded, not absorbed.
- Provenance must be preserved.
- External contributions must be attributed.
- Frameworks must not be silently collapsed into one another.

**16.2** Research findings do not automatically become AVCS doctrine. They become test results. Doctrine is amended only through the process defined in the Charter and CORE.

**16.3** Where research involves external collaborators, their contribution is preserved and cited in any derivative work.

I commit to:

- Conducting my work in accordance with the **AVCS Charter v1.1**, **CORE v2.1**, this **Code of Ethics**, and the **Code of Practice**.

- Preserving the **anonymity** of those who speak.
- Recording **convergence**, not absorbing it.
- **Declining work** where my objectivity could be questioned.
- **Accepting sanctions** if I violate this Code.

I understand that violation of this Code may result in sanctions, up to and including permanent revocation of certification.

I accept that my name, if revoked, will be removed from the directory of AVCS Practitioners.

I take this obligation freely and without reservation.
