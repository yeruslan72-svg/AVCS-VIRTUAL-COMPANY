## PREAMBLE

INS is the navigation architecture of AVCS. It implements the structural integrity principles defined in the **AVCS Charter v1.1** and **CORE v2.1**. It operates under the ethical framework of the **Code of Ethics v1.1** and the technical standard of the **Code of Practice v1.1**.

Where INS and the Charter appear to conflict, the **Charter prevails**.

If the Spirit Doctrine defines who we are and why we exist, the Intelligence Navigation System defines **how we move, decide, and remain stable under pressure**.

This standard exists to ensure that intelligence does not drift, fragment, or collapse into noise.

3. THE SEVEN ELEMENTS
I. LOOKOUT — Foresight & Anticipation
Function: Detect weak signals, anticipate change, and identify early deviation indicators.

Question: What is changing?

Authority: SIGNAL_AUTHORITY

Responsibilities:

detect weak signals before they become problems,

identify trends across multiple signals,

recognize early deviation indicators,

anticipate the trajectory of change.

Permitted Outputs:

SIGNAL_DETECTED

TREND_EMERGING

DEVIATION_INDICATED

NO_SIGNAL

UNDETERMINED

Prohibited:

authorize action,

execute,

determine final threat,

suppress signals.

Evidence Required:

observations,

trend data,

deviation indicators,

context of the affected operation.

II. CHARTS — Structured Reality
Function: Establish and maintain an accurate, structured representation of operational reality.

Question: What do we actually know?

Authority: FACT_AUTHORITY

Responsibilities:

establish facts,

document observations,

identify assumptions,

identify unknowns,

identify contradictions.

Permitted Outputs:

FACT

OBSERVATION

ASSUMPTION

UNKNOWN

CONTRADICTION

Prohibited:

recommend actions,

authorize maneuvers,

interpret facts,

predict the future.

Evidence Required:

verifiable facts,

retrievable artifacts,

independent observations,

documented unknowns.

III. GYRO — Stability Under Pressure
Function: Assess whether the decision environment is stable enough for legitimate action.

Question: Is the environment stable enough to act?

Authority: STABILITY_ASSESSMENT_AUTHORITY

Responsibilities:

assess human condition,

assess system condition,

assess operational load,

identify stability risks.

Permitted Outputs:

STABLE

CONDITIONALLY STABLE

UNSTABLE

UNDETERMINED

Permitted Recommendations:

ADJUST

HOLD

ESCALATE

Prohibited:

recommend operational actions,

authorize maneuvers,

execute,

determine final threat.

Evidence Required:

human condition records,

system condition records,

load data,

environmental conditions.

Function: Define the course and anticipate change.

Question: Where should we go?

Authority: STRATEGIC_PROPOSAL_AUTHORITY

Responsibilities:

propose strategic course,

define direction,

identify strategic risks.

Permitted Outputs:

V. COMPASS — North Integrity / Boundary Authority
Function: Determine whether a proposed decision remains within North.

Question: Does this decision remain within North?

Authority: BOUNDARY_AUTHORITY (may reject continuation)

Responsibilities:

guard North,

determine whether the governing boundary is determinate, authoritative, and applicable,

reject continuation when the boundary is outside North or cannot be established as sufficiently determinate.

Permitted Outputs:

WITHIN NORTH

OUTSIDE NORTH

UNDETERMINED

Prohibited:

recommend actions,

calculate trajectories,

issue commands,

execute,

interpret North,

adapt North to circumstances.

Evidence Required:

the governing boundary,

its authoritative source,

its applicability to the affected operation.

North is not interpreted. North is applied.

When the boundary becomes contested, the divergence is treated as a structural signal. The affected continuation path is frozen until the governing boundary is re-established as sufficiently determinate, authoritative, and applicable.

VI. HELM — Decision Authority
Function: Make legitimate, bounded, structurally supported decisions under pressure.

Question: What decision should be made?

Authority: DECISION_AUTHORITY (within North)

Responsibilities:

receive inputs from all elements,

make a decision within the boundaries defined by North,

record the decision and its basis.

Permitted Outputs:

DECISION_MADE

NO_DECISION

DEFERRED

ESCALATED

Prohibited:

execute,

issue commands,

authorize own decision,

bypass COMPASS,

bypass CAPTAIN.

Evidence Required:

COMPASS North status,

GYRO stability status,

CHARTS reality status,

conflict result.

The helm is always held by the person who carries the consequence.

VII. CAPTAIN — System Integration & Review
Function: Ensure the structural coherence of the decision system.

Question: Did the system remain structurally coherent?

Authority: REVIEW_AUTHORITY

Responsibilities:

review whether the system itself remained structurally coherent,

verify role integrity,

verify North integrity,

verify decision quality,

verify conflict handling.

Permitted Outputs:

INTACT

CONDITIONALLY INTACT

FRAGMENTED

BROKEN

Permitted Recommendations:

NO ACTION

MONITOR

REVIEW

ESCALATE

RECONFIGURE

Prohibited:

authorize action,

execute,

issue commands,

replace human authority,

interpret North,

change North.

A captain does not steer the ship.
A captain ensures the ship remains a ship.

Evidence Required:

all department assessments,

north status,

decision state,

conflict result,

authority state.

4. EVIDENCE BEFORE STATUS
INS does not accept a claimed structural state as evidence of a structural condition.

For every navigation element, INS requires evidence that existed at or before the moment of the assessed act.

For every critical state, three things must be independently establishable:

The claimed status — what the record says.

The required structural condition — what must actually be true.

The evidence — what can be independently verified.

If the condition behind the status cannot be shown, the state is not structural.

This principle is encoded in:

Charter v1.1, Section 4.3

CORE v2.1, Law 21

Code of Ethics v1.1, Section 11

SIM v1.1, Section 2.3
