# AVCS PRACTITIONER COUNCIL CHARTER

## Governance of the AVCS Practitioner Community

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Institutional Charter — Frozen
**Effective Date:** September 2026
**Companion Documents:** AVCS Charter v1.1, CORE v2.1, Code of Ethics v1.1, Code of Practice v1.1

---

## PREAMBLE

This Charter establishes the **AVCS Practitioner Council** — the governing body of the AVCS Practitioner community.

Until the Council is formally established, its functions are carried out by the Founder, except in matters concerning the Founder.

The Council exists to:

- protect the integrity of AVCS as a discipline,
- govern the certification of Practitioners,
- adjudicate violations of the Code of Ethics and Code of Practice,
- preserve the Constitutional foundation of AVCS,
- ensure that AVCS remains testable against its own governing principles.

The Council is not a marketing body.

It is not a commercial entity.

It is the **structural guardian** of AVCS.

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Council exists to guard the Charter as North.

**The Charter is the North of AVCS.**

**It is not interpreted. It is applied.**

The Council is the **structural guardian of this boundary**.

The Practitioner Council exists to:

1. Maintain the integrity of the AVCS Charter, CORE, Code of Ethics, and Code of Practice.
2. Govern the certification, recertification, and revocation of Practitioners.
3. Review alleged violations of the Code of Ethics and Code of Practice.
4. Adjudicate appeals from certified Practitioners.
5. Maintain the public directory of certified AVCS Practitioners.
6. Amend the Code of Ethics and Code of Practice when required.
7. Preserve provenance where AVCS adopts principles from external convergence.
8. Ensure that AVCS remains subject to its own structural integrity principles.

---

## 2. AUTHORITY

The Council derives its authority from the **AVCS Charter v1.1**.

The Council is subordinate to the Charter.

Where the Council's decisions conflict with the Charter, the **Charter prevails**.

**The Council does not have authority to interpret the Charter.**

**The Council has authority to apply the Charter.**

Where the Charter is silent, the Council may establish procedures.

Where the Charter speaks, the Council enforces.

The Council does not have authority to:

- amend the Charter unilaterally,
- redefine the foundational principles of AVCS,
- override the requirement for evidence in matters of ethical review,
- silence dissent within the Practitioner community,
- interpret North.

---

## 3. COMPOSITION

### 3.1 Founding Phase

During the initial phase (first 10 certified Practitioners), the functions of the Council are carried out by the Founder.

This transitional governance is acknowledged as necessary but provisional.

### 3.2 Established Phase

Once the first 10 Practitioners are certified, the Council is formally established.

The Council consists of:

- **Three senior Practitioners**, selected by the Practitioner community.
- **One external advisor**, selected by the Council for domain expertise.
- **The Founder**, as a non-voting member (except in matters of procedure).

### 3.3 Selection of Council Members

Senior Practitioners are selected by:

- nomination by at least two certified Practitioners,
- confirmation by simple majority of the certified community,
- a term of three years, renewable once.

### 3.4 Chair

The Council elects a Chair from among its senior Practitioner members.

The Chair serves a one-year term, renewable once.

The Chair has no additional authority beyond procedural leadership.

---

## 4. FUNCTIONS

### 4.1 Certification

The Council:

- approves the certification process defined in the Code of Practice,
- reviews candidate eligibility,
- confirms successful completion of certification requirements,
- maintains the public directory of certified Practitioners.

### 4.2 Recertification

The Council:

- reviews evidence of completed SIM audits,
- verifies continued adherence to the Code of Ethics,
- confirms recertification every three years.

### 4.3 Violations

The Council:

- reviews alleged violations of the Code of Ethics and Code of Practice,
- provides the accused Practitioner with the right to respond,
- determines sanctions in accordance with the Code of Ethics, Section 8.

### 4.4 Appeals

The Council:

- receives appeals from certified Practitioners,
- reviews the basis of the appeal,
- provides a written decision with reasoning.

### 4.5 Amendments

The Council may amend the Code of Ethics and Code of Practice by two-thirds majority.

Substantive changes require:

- documentation of the reason for change,
- identification of the source architecture (if from joint work or external convergence),
- preservation of provenance,
- communication to all active Practitioners,
- reaffirmation by all active Practitioners.

### 4.6 Charter Amendments

The Council may propose amendments to the Charter.

Charter amendments require:

- a documented proposal from at least three senior Practitioners,
- review by the Council,
- two-thirds majority approval,
- confirmation that the amendment does not contradict the Spirit Doctrine or the foundational principles of AVCS.

The Charter may not be amended to remove or weaken:

- Human Sovereignty.
- North is applied, not interpreted.
- A status is not a structural condition.
- Evidence ex ante.
- Code implements the Constitution — not redefines it.

---

## 5. PROCEDURES

### 5.1 Meetings

The Council meets:

- at least twice per year, in ordinary session,
- additionally, when convened by the Chair or by request of at least two members,
- in extraordinary session, when required by urgent matters.

### 5.2 Quorum

A quorum consists of at least three voting members.

Decisions require a simple majority of those present, except where otherwise specified.

### 5.3 Records

The Council maintains records of:

- all decisions,
- all sanctions,
- all appeals,
- all amendments,
- all matters of provenance.

Records are preserved for at least seven years.

### 5.4 Confidentiality

The Council respects the confidentiality of:

- individual Practitioners under review,
- client organizations,
- informants in audited systems.

Reasons for sanctions remain confidential unless the Practitioner chooses to disclose them.

---

## 6. ETHICAL OBLIGATIONS OF THE COUNCIL

The Council is bound by the **Code of Ethics v1.1** in the same manner as every certified Practitioner.

The Council must:

- require evidence, not accept status,
- apply North, not interpret it,
- record convergence, not absorb it,
- treat divergence as a test result, not an automatic change,
- preserve the anonymity of those who speak,
- act in service of the integrity of AVCS.

---

## 7. FOUNDER'S ROLE

### 7.1 Founding Phase

During the initial phase (first 10 certified Practitioners), the Founder carries out the functions of the Council, except in matters concerning the Founder.

### 7.2 Established Phase

After the Council is established, the Founder:

- is a non-voting member of the Council,
- retains the right to propose amendments,
- retains the right to address the Council on matters of significance,
- does not hold veto authority over Council decisions,
- is bound by the Charter in the same manner as every certified Practitioner.

### 7.3 Allegations Against the Founder

Allegations against the Founder are reviewed by a panel of three senior Practitioners selected by the Council.

If the Founder is found to have violated the Code of Ethics, the same sanctions apply — including potential revocation of Founder privileges in community governance.

**The Founder is not exempt from the Charter.**

**The Charter is North. North is applied to everyone.**

---

## 8. DIRECTORY OF CERTIFIED PRACTITIONERS

The Council maintains a public directory of certified AVCS Practitioners.

The directory includes:

- name,
- certification date,
- recertification date,
- status (active / suspended / revoked).

The directory does not include:

- audit findings,
- client information,
- reasons for sanctions.

Where a Practitioner's certification is revoked, their name is removed from the directory.

---

## 9. RELATIONSHIP TO EXTERNAL CONVERGENCE

Where AVCS adopts a principle, formulation, or structural distinction that originated outside AVCS, the Council must preserve the origin.

**Convergence is recorded, not absorbed.**

The Council maintains records of:

- external contributions,
- joint working notes,
- boundary tests with external architectures,
- independent convergence identified between AVCS and other frameworks.

These records are preserved in the Council's archive and are available for review.

---

## 10. RELATIONSHIP TO THE VIRTUAL COMPANY

The Council recognizes the **AVCS Virtual Company** as a reference implementation of the AVCS decision architecture.

The Council may from time to time commission constitutional compliance tests of the Virtual Company, in accordance with CORE v2.1, Law 23.

The results of such tests are recorded as **test results** — not as automatic changes to the Charter or CORE.

**If implementation diverges from Constitution:**

- The divergence is recorded.
- The Constitution remains unchanged.
- The implementation is treated as non-conforming until formally reconciled.

---

## 11. AMENDMENT OF THIS CHARTER

This Charter may be amended by:

- the Founder, during the initial phase,
- the Council, by two-thirds majority, thereafter.

Substantive changes require:

- documentation of the reason for change,
- preservation of provenance,
- communication to all active Practitioners.
